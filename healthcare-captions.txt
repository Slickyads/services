============================================================
QUICK USAGE NOTES (CLINIC-SPECIFIC)
============================================================

- Target the CONDITION, not the clinic ("Dental implants Dubai Marina" 5x better than "Best dental clinic Dubai")
- WhatsApp or "Book appointment" button — NOT 8-field forms
- UAE patients want to ask questions (price, insurance, location) before booking
- Track cost per BOOKED appointment, not cost per lead
- Geo-fence to 15-min drive catchment (not whole city)
- One condition per ad → one condition per landing page (no multi-service pages)
- Use carousel format for educational clinic content
- Use Reels format for patient testimonial videos
- Avoid before/after medical photos on Instagram (shadow-banned by Meta)


============================================================
POSTING BEST PRACTICES (UAE healthcare 2026)
============================================================

- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 7-9 AM (parents before work) or 8-10 PM (after kids sleep)
- Avoid: Friday morning (weekend), Saturday late night
- Compliance: include DHA / DOH license number in bio or page
- Never use patient photos without written consent
- Hashtags: 15 per post (rotate vertical-specific + core)


============================================================
WHAT TO AVOID
============================================================

- Before/after medical photos (Meta shadow-bans these in UAE)
- Promising specific medical outcomes in ad copy
- Targeting by medical condition (Meta policy restriction)
- Cold audience retargeting with patient data
- Banned tags: #transformation #weightlossjourney (health verticals)
