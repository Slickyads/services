05 — CASE STUDY

An AED 145M launch, sold out in 88 days

Meridian Developments · 328-unit premium tower · Business Bay, Dubai. Shared with permission, figures reconciled against their books.
THE PROBLEM

Three agencies before us. AED 240 CPL and a 4% viewing rate. Leads scattered across agents with no follow-up discipline — marketing looked "busy," but nobody could say which channel actually produced a booking.

WEEK 1–2 · REBUILD THE FUNNEL

Tracking first, spend second.

Server-side tracking and Conversion API wired to every touchpoint. One CRM, auto-assigning leads within 90 seconds. Every campaign moved to click-to-WhatsApp as the primary capture path.

WEEK 3–8 · SCALE WHAT CONVERTED

CPL fell while spend tripled.

Creative rotated against qualified leads, not clicks. CPL dropped from AED 240 to AED 118 as monthly spend scaled from AED 12K to AED 42K. Viewing rate climbed from 4% to 14% inside six weeks.

WEEK 9–12 · CLOSE THE LOOP

Bookings taught the ad account.

Every booking fed back into attribution, and budget shifted weekly toward what actually converted. Automated no-show chase lifted the show-up rate by 30%. The launch sold out on day 88.

07 — FAQ

The questions every developer asks first

What does a lead actually cost?+

Blended CPL across our live Dubai accounts runs AED 118–240 depending on project tier, area and creative maturity. You'll get a number specific to your project before any spend commitment, not a generic range.

How fast until we see leads?+

Campaigns typically launch within 5–7 business days of the audit. First qualified leads land inside the first week; the funnel keeps improving over the first 30–45 days as creative and audiences get tested.

Do you replace our CRM?+

Only if you want us to. Most clients keep their existing CRM (HubSpot, Zoho, Bitrix24, Kommo) — we configure scoring, sequencing and WhatsApp integration inside it rather than migrating you to something new.

How does the WhatsApp part work?+

Click-to-WhatsApp ads and forms route directly into WhatsApp Business API, so the first reply goes out automatically while intent is still hot, then hands off to a sales agent once a lead is qualified.

What's the minimum budget?+

We generally recommend AED 25,000/month minimum in ad spend to generate a statistically useful funnel — enough volume to test creative and optimise toward bookings rather than guesswork.

Will you work with our competitors?+

We cap how many developments we take on per micro-market and price tier at any one time, and we'll tell you upfront if that's a conflict before you sign anything.

What do we see in reporting?+

A weekly dashboard covering CPL, connect rate, qualification rate, viewings booked and pipeline value by campaign and audience — attributed back to spend, not just impressions and clicks.

What are the contract terms?+

A 90-day initial pilot, month-to-month after that. We'd rather earn the renewal every quarter than lock you into a term that outlives our results.
