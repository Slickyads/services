# Client Results Snapshot — Tabchilli

**Slicky Media | Performance Marketing**

---

## The Situation

Tabchilli was running Meta Ads across multiple campaigns with no clear visibility into where spend was actually working — a common blind spot when campaigns are set up and left to run without regular auditing.

## What We Found

A full account audit (June spend period) revealed:

- **~36% of ad spend** was going to campaigns performing below a 0.85x ROAS threshold — effectively losing money on over a third of the budget
- Google Ads spend showed a **budget utilization gap** — allocated budget wasn't being fully or efficiently deployed
- Performance Max campaigns were showing **brand bleed** — cannibalizing branded search traffic that would have converted anyway, inflating perceived PMax performance

## What We Did

- Identified and flagged underperforming campaigns for pause, creative refresh, or reallocation
- Rebuilt budget allocation logic to close the utilization gap in Google Ads
- Corrected PMax brand bleed to get a true read on incremental performance
- Delivered findings via a structured audit (10-tab performance tracker) built specifically for recurring monthly review — not a one-off report

## The Result

*Reallocating the 36% of wasted spend into top-performing campaigns Cost-per-result dropped from AED 90 to AED 65 over 60 days.*

---

## Why This Matters for You

If you're running Meta or Google Ads without a recurring audit process, there's a good chance a similar percentage of your budget is quietly underperforming right now — most accounts we review have this same pattern. This is exactly the kind of gap a generic ad-management service won't catch, because it requires actively auditing spend against ROAS thresholds, not just "managing" campaigns.
